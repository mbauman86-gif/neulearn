What a Buddy Profile Looks Like (from another kid’s view)
Think of it like a little “trading card + mini-room”:

Top section (Header)

Big avatar

Display name / nickname (no last names)

Small tag line like:

“Explorer • Age 7 • Loves Animals & Space”
(auto-generated from their settings, not free text)

Theme & background

Full-width background based on the theme they chose:

Forest, Space, Ocean, Castle, Cozy Cabin, etc.

Theme subtly changes:

Background art

Accent colors

Maybe header pattern

Info section

About Me block (SAFE, structured)

“I feel most like:” → [ Maker | Reader | Artist | Explorer | Builder | Leader ]

“I like to learn by:” → [ Building | Drawing | Moving | Listening | Reading stories ]

“3 fun facts about me” → they choose from a list like:

“I love animals”

“I like Lego”

“I’m learning to read”

“I like helping my friends”
(No open text; all from pre-written options.)

Favorite Subjects

Icons + chips: [ Reading | Math | Science | Nature | Art | Building | Stories ]

They can pick 2–4, and you show them as cute colored tags.

Top Buddies

“My Top Buddies” row (like old-school top friends, but small & kind):

3–5 buddy avatars with names

Tap → go to that buddy’s profile

Recent Activity / Highlights

“Recent Adventures” section:

A small grid or list of:

Last 3 badges earned

Last 3 completed quests/challenges

Card examples:

“Finished Reading Quest: Silly Animals”

“Earned Builder Badge”

“Submitted Lego Tower to Weekly Challenge”

Reactions

At the bottom:

Buttons like: ⭐ Nice! / 🎉 So cool! / 💡 Inspiring!

When a buddy taps, it shows a tiny “{BuddyName} reacted ⭐” on your side.

2️⃣ What the Profile Owner Can Customize
This is your MySpace‑energy part, but controlled.

A. Theme & Layout
From “Edit Profile” screen:

Choose Theme:

Forest, Space, Ocean, Castle, City, Farm, Cozy Room…

Choose Accent Color within the theme (limited palette):

E.g. for Space: teal / purple / blue

Optional later:

Pick layout style:

“Classic” (info first, then activity)

“Showcase” (activity first, then info)

“Compact” (simple card-style)

B. About Me (but safe)
Instead of free typing, you give them builders:

“I feel most like…”
Choose up to 2 identity tags:

Maker, Reader, Artist, Scientist, Builder, Leader, Explorer

“I like to learn by…”
Choose up to 2:

Building, Drawing, Moving, Listening, Talking, Reading, Playing games

“Fun facts about me…”
Choose up to 3 from a pool:

“I love animals”

“I like to build things”

“I like helping people”

“I love stories”

“I’m learning to read”

“I like being outside”

“I like math puzzles”
etc.

You can ALWAYS add more later.

C. Favorite Subjects
Checkbox list → displayed as tags:

Reading

Math

Science

Nature

Art

Music

Building / Engineering

Stories / Writing

They pick 2–4. You enforce min/max in the UI.

D. Top Friends (Top Buddies)
In “Edit profile”:

Show a list of their buddies with toggles:

 Show in “Top Buddies”

Limit to 3–5 showing at once.

Order can be:

Most often interacted with, or

Allow them to pick (but still from approved buddy list).

Parents should be able to override this from parent dashboard if needed.
